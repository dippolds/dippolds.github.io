Several companies on [Amazon sell a hidden AppleTV mount](http://www.amazon.com/TotalMount-Apple-Universal-Mounting-Kit/dp/B0057CVH6W). The mounts allows the AppleTV to be attached behind the screen to improve the aesthetics of your TV. The Apple remote control will continue to work as long as the front of the AppleTV faces up to take at reflected IR signals from the remote. Alternatively, you can make your own mount.

## Option 1 - Cheesy but only 5 minutes

 Tie the AppleTV to the vents. Use non-metallic ties and loop them carefully in the vents with the TV unplugged to avoid shock and damage. Clearly, this approach won't hold up with any TV movement.
