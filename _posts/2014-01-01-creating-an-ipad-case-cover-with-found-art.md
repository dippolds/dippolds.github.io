A cheap iPad mini case from Five-Below seems like a good deal until you realize itlacks character. 1. Find some art you like from a magazine. 2. Lightly spray the case with adhesive cement. 3. Place the art on the case and rub firmly. 4. Trim the excess of the art with a sharp knife.
