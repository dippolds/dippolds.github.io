We discovered the lightly touristed town of Ridgway while attending a [wedding in nearby St. Marys](http://www.stmaryssportsmen.org/), PA. The [Towers Inn](http://www.ncentral.com/~towers/) is a Victorian-era mansion loaded with charm and history and is well-worth the stay. The town of Ridgway contains many similar mansions and quant shopping. Just outside of town is the vast Allegheny National Forest containing 500,000 acres of hike-able trails.
