## Public transport

 **4 1/2 to 5 1/2 hours**, **~$34 one-way** Septa 93 bus to Norristown transportation center $2.25 (hourly) R6 train to 30th Street Station $7 at peak (hourly) BoltBus to NYC $22 (varies $18-24, hourly to every 30 minutes) Subway in NYC $2.50 ## By Car
