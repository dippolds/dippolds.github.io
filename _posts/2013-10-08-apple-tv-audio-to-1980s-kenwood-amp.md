The 45 watt [Kenwood KA-400 integrated amp](http://www.radiomuseum.org/r/trio_kenwo_dc_stereo_integrated_amp.html) purchased in the early 1980's for junior-high DJ'ing still works and sits in a kitchen to the stylish grimaces of its occupants. It almost went to be recycled due to the absence of a digital input needed by the AppleTV. [![20131008-211735.jpg](/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/20131008-211735.jpg)](/wp-content/uploads/2013/10/20131008-211735.jpg) A cheap replacement amp with digital input and couldn't be found new for less than $150. Instead, a [$18 digital optical coax to RCA](http://www.amazon.com/Digital-Optical-Analog-Converter-Adapter/dp/B009GER8A6) and [$6 optical cable](http://www.amazon.com/AmazonBasics-Digital-Optical-Audio-Toslink/dp/B001TH7GSW) solved the problem to the chagrin of others in the house. As the Kenwood has a switched outlet, there is no waste in power when not in use.
