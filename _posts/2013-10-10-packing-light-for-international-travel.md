Maximize the enjoyment of your trip by minimizing what you pack.

# Method

 One carry-on bag is sufficient for 1-3 weeks of travel and maybe more. The key is packing clothes that work in any combination, layering, and doing some washing in the sink or in an apartment or hotel during the trip. A second, smaller bag serves as a day pack and to keep your valuables close yet it should fit in the carry-on bag if needed. # Packing List

 Put following in something like a [Red Oxx carry-on bag](http://www.redoxx.com/Airline-Carry-on-Luggage/Air-Boss/91018/100/Product). - Instead of a day pack, bring 1 large purse of light, durable, yet stylish design like [Elaine Arsenault](http://www.elainearsenault.com/).
- Neutral colors like black can both look formal or casual and help minimize clothes items. Remember, 3 tops and 3 bottoms equals 9 outfits.
- If you want to bring something back, consider clothes that go only 1-way. In other words, take old underwear and sock and discard them during the trip.
- Lightweight scarf for warmth or modesty in Muslim countries.
- Bring only comfortable shoes including 1 pair of comfortable tennis shoes.
