Amsterdam and W. 96th. [<span style="text-decoration: underline;">http://www.divebarnyc.com/</span>](http://www.divebarnyc.com/) Unwined: Wine bar on W. 95th between Broadway and West End Avenue. [<span style="text-decoration: underline;">http://www.telepan-ny.com/</span>](http://www.telepan-ny.com/) Red Cat - Generous portions of creative, wholesome New American cuisine in Chelsea. 10th Ave. and W. 23rd. W. 4th at W. 12th. [<span style="text-decoration: underline;">http://www.cafecluny.com/</span>](http://www.cafecluny.com/) <span style="text-decoration: underline;">Union Square Cafe</span> - A NY institution near Union Square. E. 16th between Union Square and 5th Ave. [<span style="text-decoration: underline;">www.unionsquarecafe.com/</span>](http://www.unionsquarecafe.com/)
