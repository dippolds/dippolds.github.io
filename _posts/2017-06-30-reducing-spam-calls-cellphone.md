In the last year a lot more robo and telemarketing calls are going to cellphones. There are several things you can do to reduce these nuisance calls.

1. **Most effective** - install an app like [Call Protect](https://www.att.com/offers/call-protect.html). This app is free for AT&amp;T users and actively warns you a call is a telemarketer or spam. [![](/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/call_protect-169x300.png)](/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/call_protect.png)
2. Verizon [takes a different approach](https://wbillpay.verizonwireless.com/vzw/nos/uc/uc_spam_control.jsp) that doesn't require an app but requires activation.
3. Register your number on the [National Do Not Call list](https://www.donotcall.gov/). This works for companies that follow the rules and check the list before calling. Unfortunately, many companies ignore the list as penalties are limited.
4. **Least effective** - Block the number using your cellphones built-in blocker. Since this is based on the caller's phone number which may be spoofed or not re-used, this doesn't suppress to many callers.
