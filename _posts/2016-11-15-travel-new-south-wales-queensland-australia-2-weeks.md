This is an [overview of a 2 week trip through New South Wales and Queensland](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7wFV2hjReOc). The trip included Sydney, the Blue Mountains, Symbio Wildlife Park, Kiama, Raymond Island, Tilba, Phillip Island, Broken Hill, Silverton, and Melbourne. https://youtu.be/7wFV2hjReOc Most of the travel was by rental car. A flight was taken to get from Melbourne to Broken Hill to get a taste of the outback.
