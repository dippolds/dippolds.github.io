Three days is sufficient time to sample the highlights of Mumbai. Tourism usually starts in Colaba at the southern tip of the city. [The video covers the key sights of the Mumbai](https://youtu.be/mZXuUnB61fM) including India Gate, the Jain temple and Hanging Gardens of Malabar Hill, a Dhobi Ghat (clothes washing), the Ghandi House museum, Dharavi slum, Sanjay Gandhi National Park, the Kanheri caves, and Crawford market. \[embedyt\] http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mZXuUnB61fM\[/embedyt\] While not covered in the video, recommended places to eat include Shamiana restaurant in the Taj Mahal Palace Hotel, and the Konkan Cafe at the Vivanta by Taj Hotel. \[caption id="attachment\_1061" align="alignright" width="640"\]![Monkey on a cycle](/assets/img/uploads/2017/02/DSC_4156-1024x678.jpg) Monkey on a cycle\[/caption\]
