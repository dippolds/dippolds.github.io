A squirrel chewed through a fuel line on an outdoor generator requiring a replacement. The cheapest and easiest source was eBay as there are suppliers who sell by the foot.

## Steps

 1. Use a micrometer or caliper to meassure the inner diameter (I.D.) and outer diameter (O.D.) of the damaged fuel line. 2. Since most precise measurement devices use decimal/metric inches (thousandth of an inch), use [a conversion table](http://www.hamuniverse.com/antfrac.html) to get the sizes in the closest fractional inches. 3. If the fuel line is old, you may not find the same outer dimension by searching eBay (example [fuel line 3/16" I.D.](http://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_trksid=p2050601.m570.l1313.TR0.TRC0.H0.Xfuel+line+3%2F16%22+I.D.&_nkw=fuel+line+3%2F16%22+I.D.&_sacat=0&_from=R40)). If so, make sure you also get replacement fuel clips (example [fuel line clips 3/16"](http://www.ebay.com/sch/i.html?_odkw=fuel+line+3%2F16%22+I.D.&_osacat=0&_from=R40&_trksid=p2045573.m570.l1313.TR0.TRC0.H0.Xfuel+line+clips+3%2F16%22&_nkw=fuel+line+clips+3%2F16%22&_sacat=0)). Select a seller that sells by the foot and order slightly more than you need as most of the cost is for shipping.
