[Wholey's Fish Market](http://www.wholey.com/) in Pittsburgh is the best seafood store I have seen outside of [Tsukiji fish market](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsukiji_fish_market) in Tokyo (and that is really for wholesale market). The fishmongers really know their stuff. This worth a stop if you are in the strip district of Pittsburgh.
