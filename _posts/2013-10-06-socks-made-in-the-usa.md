## Objective

 Find everyday dress socks that are made in the USA. Previously, Gold Toe had many lines made in the USA but they are down to just two. ## Requirements

- Made in the USA
- Black or Tan
- Not too thick, not too thin.
- &lt;$5 ea. including shipping
 
## Options
