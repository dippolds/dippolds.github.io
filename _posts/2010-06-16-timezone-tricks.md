Working with people in different time zones presents multiple problems. Answering the simple questions of what time is it now and what time is it at a future date and time can be confusing. **Trick 1 - Web solution** http://www.timeanddate.com/ Here is an example of a link that will show a future time for the major cities worldwide [<span style="text-decoration: underline;">http://www.timeanddate.com/<wbr></wbr>worldclock/fixedtime.html?<wbr></wbr>month=6&amp;day=7&amp;year=2010&amp;hour=<wbr></wbr>10&amp;min=0&amp;sec=0&amp;p1=179</span>](http://www.timeanddate.com/worldclock/fixedtime.html?month=6&day=7&year=2010&hour=10&min=0&sec=0&p1=179) **Trick 2 - Windows utility** A useful desktop clock called [Qlock](http://www.qlock.com/) allows you to see the current time in cities of your choice as well as picking a future date and time.
