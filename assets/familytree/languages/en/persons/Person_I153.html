Notes
Rowland joined the militia so would not have been approved by the DarbyMeeting.

He was assistant Marshall - 1790 Census Delaware County, Market Street.

1811 Tavern keeper, Philadelphia. 1816-1819 ran the Schuykill Hotel onFront and High St.

1819 manager of Washington Tavern. 1824 Inkeeper N 5th. 1830 Inspectorof Customs.

He also had a cordage business.
