Notes
Freiderich immigrated to the port of Boston on 15 Sep 1852. According to a newspaper account, he immigrated in 1854. He first settled in Worcester, Mass, for a short time. He moved to St. Mary's, PA in 1855, where he purchased a farm. He applied for citizenship on 13 Jun 1856, and was naturalized on 2 Jul 1860.
