HOOD, THOMAS. Darby, yeoman.
September 7, 1717. August 7, 1718. A. 60.
To eldest daughter Sarah wife of David Thomas £50. To daughter Hannah wife of George Wood, £50. To daughter Ann, wife of Enoch Bonsall, £50. To daughter Mary £50. To daughter Martha wife of Jacob Bonsall, £50. Land and plantation in Darby to be sold for the use of said 5 daughters children. Executors to make title to the 200 acres of land in Abingdon sold to Geo. Godshett.
Executors: sons in law, David Thomas, George Wood, Jacob and Enoch
Bonsall. Brother John Hood and his son Jonathan to be overseers.
