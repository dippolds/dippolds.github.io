BONSALL, DAVID. Springfield.
January 28, 1778. September 22, 1784.
Executors to sell my undivided half part of plantation whereon I now dwell in Springfield. To my 5 brothers, Benjamin, Enoch, Joseph, Joshua and Jonathan and 2 sisters Sarah and Hannah £210 to be equally divided. To Mary and George, children of my brother Isaac Bonsall, deceassed, £30 each at 21. To Samuel, son of brother Enoch, £10 at 21, with rev. to his sister Ann. To John, son of brother Jonathan, £10 at 21. To Hannah, widow of brother Isaac, £15. To Elizabeth, brother Benjamin's wife £15. To Rachel, brother Enoch's wife, £15. To Lydia, wife of brother Joseph, £15. Remainder to 5 brothers and 2 sisters, above named. Executors: Brother Benjamin and near kinsman Abraham Booth. Letters to Bonsall, the other being deceased.
Wit: John Hibberd, Isaac Hibberd, Jr., Isaac Pearson.To Mary and George, children of my brother Isaac Bonsall, deceassed, £30 each at 21.To Hannah, widow of brother Isaac, £15.To Elizabeth, brother Benjamin's wife £15.To Lydia, wife of brother Joseph, £15.To Rachel, brother Enoch's wife, £15.
