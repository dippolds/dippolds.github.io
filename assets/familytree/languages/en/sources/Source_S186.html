MORRIS, JONATHAN. Marple, yeoman.
September 12, 1741. October 1, 1741. B. 96.
Provides for wife Katherine. To youngest son Samuel my plantation whereon I live when 21, paying to his sister Phebe and Hannah £20 each. To eldest son Jonathan remainder of land east of Radnor road, when of age, paying to his sister Mary £20.
Executors: wife and brother in law Francis Yarnall.
Witnesses: Mordecai Morris, John Morris, Mirick Davis.
