FEARN, JOSIAH. Darby.
March 10, 1730. March 30, 1731. A. 330.
To daughter Sarah Bunting 5 shillings. To daughter Rebecca Fearn £100 also lott of ground when 21. To granddaughter Elizabeth Hibbert £10 when 21. To grandaughter Mary Bunting £5 when 21. To son Josiah all remainder of estate real and personal.
Executors: friend Saml. Sellus and son Josiah.
