Sisters: Hannah Price, Sarah Elliot. Cousins: Edward Bonsall, Martha (daughter of Philip and Hannah Price). Brothers: Benjamin and Nathan.
Overseers: Jonathan Paschall (uncle), cousin John Hunt. Exec:
Brother Benjamin.
