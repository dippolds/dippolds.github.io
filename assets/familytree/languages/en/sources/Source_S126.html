WOOD, HANNAH. Widow. Darby.
February 29, 1772. January 18, 1773.
To 3 daughters Jane, Sarah and Ann all wearing apparel. All remainder of estate to 4 children, viz., Jonathan, Jane, Sarah and Ann and to the 5 children of son George Wood deceased, viz., Hannah, Margaret, Mary, Henry and Harriet.
