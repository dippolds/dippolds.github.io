TAYLOR, THOMAS. Springfield.
August 12, 1725. December 28, 1725. A. 186.
Executor to sell plantation where I now live when youngest son Isaac is 14. To my 4 sons, Thomas, Mordecai, Jonathan and Isaac, £20 each. To my mother Mary Selby £5. To daughters Mary, Susanna, Esther and Hannah, articles named. Remainder to above named 8 children.
Executors: brothers Barth Coppeock of Marple and Henry Lewis of Haverford. Witnesses: Robt. Taylor, Jo.
