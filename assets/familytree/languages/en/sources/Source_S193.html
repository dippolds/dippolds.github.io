Pennsylvania Wills, 1682-1834

BARTRAM, ELIZABETH. Widow. Marple.
October 30, 1771. December 17, 1771.
To granddaughter Elizabeth wife of Isaac Maris furniture. To granddaughter Eliza Bartram wife of John Bartram, Jr. furniture and £100 at 21. To granddaughter Sarah Howell household goods. To my nurse Margaret Easton £10. To great grandchild Mary Maris £5. To great grandchild Elizabeth Maris £5 at 21. Remainder to 3 grandchildren Elizabeth Maris, Eliza Bartram and Sarah Howell.
Executors: Lewis Davis and James Rhoads.
Wit: Seth Pancoast, Elisha Worrall, Isa. Pearson.
