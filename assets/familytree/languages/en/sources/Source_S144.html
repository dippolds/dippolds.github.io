Pennsylvania Wills, 1682-1834

RHOADS, ABIGAIL. Marple.
June 1, 1750. February 1, 1750/1. C. 264.
To eldest son John and youngest son James Rhoades, granddaughter Elizabeth Powell and her sister Abigail, articles named. To each of her 15 grandchildren £5, granddaughter Jean Rhoades named. To daughter Rebecca Massey £5. Remainder to her 5 children, viz., John and James Rhoades, Rebecca Massey, and children of Mary Powell and Benjamin Rhoades. Test signed A. Executors: Sons John and James.Wit: Mordecai Morris, James Batten.
