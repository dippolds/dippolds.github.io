PEARSON, THOMAS. Darby.
January 4, 1763. January 31, 1763.
To daughter Ann, wife of Davis Morris £186 making with what I have advanced her £250. To son James £30 making with what he owes me £250. To son John, tanyard with land there unto belonging and lot adjoining in Darby, also lot bought of estate of Job Harvey, tanning and currying tools - paying to my estate £100. To daughter Sarah £250. To daughters Susanna, Mary, Hannah, and Elizabeth £250 each. To father Benjamin Pearson the house wherein he now lives in Darby during life. To son Thomas messuage where I dwell in Darby with lots of land
adjacent, at 21 or decease of my wife. Provides for wife Hannah.
Executors: Wife and sons James and John.
