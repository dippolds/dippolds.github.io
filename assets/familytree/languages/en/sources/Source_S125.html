SELLERS, SAMUEL. W. Bradford.
December 26, 1785. January 14, 1786.
Provides for wife Jane. To 2 sons Samuel and Jonathan all my land in W. Bradford, containing 200 acres. Remainder at wife's decease to 4 daughters, Hannah Peirce, Sarah Wickersham, Mary Trimble and Ann Taylor. Executor: Son Samuel.
